Install Signature in Gmail in 60 Seconds

Follow these easy steps to install your professional signature with pixel-perfect alignment.

1

Generate & Copy Your Signature

Customize your personal information, colors, logo, and layout on the Signature Studio tab, then click the "Copy Signature" button.

2

Open Gmail Settings

Open Gmail on your computer. In the top right corner, click the Settings icon (⚙️), then click "See all settings".

3

Navigate to the Signature Section

In the General tab, scroll down until you find the Signature section. Click "+ Create new" and give your signature a name (e.g. Work Signature).

4

Paste Your Signature

Click inside the signature text editing box on the right. Press Ctrl + V (or Cmd + V on Mac) to paste. Your signature will appear with all logos, styling, and clickable links intact.

5

Set Defaults & Save

Under Signature defaults, set your new signature for "For new emails use" and "On reply/forward use". Scroll to the bottom of the page and click "Save Changes". That's it!

Direct Computer Upload vs Google Drive

You can either paste public Google Drive links or directly click "Upload Logo" / "Upload Photo" from your computer. Our engine supports both methods seamlessly with instant fallback resolution.
